## 🚀 **How to Use the New Features**

### **Flashcards**

1. **Input Notes**: Paste your study material in the enhanced textarea
2. **Generate**: Click the rocket button to create smart flashcards
3. **Navigate**: Use Previous/Next buttons or click cards to flip
4. **Reset**: Create new flashcards with the "New Flashcards" button

### **Quiz**

1. **Input Text**: Paste content to generate quiz questions
2. **Answer Questions**: Click on answer options (A, B, C, D)
3. **See Explanations**: Get detailed explanations after each answer
4. **View Results**: See your final score and percentage

### **Study Buddy**

1. **Ask Questions**: Type your learning questions
2. **Get AI Responses**: Receive intelligent study assistance
3. **Chat History**: View your conversation history
4. **Interactive Learning**: Engage in ongoing learning conversations
